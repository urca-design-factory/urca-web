# CONTENT-ARCHITECTURE.md

## Urca Design Factory — Website Content Architecture

## 1. Primary Goal

The website should establish Urca as a global, end-to-end design and technology partner.

A visitor should understand three things quickly:

1. Urca can shape an idea strategically.
2. Urca can design the brand and product.
3. Urca can actually build and evolve it.

The website should sell **capability and thinking**, not a long list of services.

# Homepage

## 01 — Hero

### Purpose

Immediately communicate positioning and establish the visual character.

### Primary headline

**We design ideas,  
we build experiences.**

Typography may use a contrast between roman and italic serif.

### Supporting copy

**Brand identities, AI-powered products, software and interactive experiences — from first idea to launch and beyond.**

### Metadata

Possible:

`URCA / DESIGN FACTORY`

`BRAND / PRODUCT / TECHNOLOGY`

`BUCHAREST — WORLDWIDE`

Location wording should be reviewed before production if the brand wants to present a specific headquarters.

### Graphic

Large generative ASCII sculpture.

The ASCII graphic should feel like a living visual object rather than an illustration of a literal object.

### CTA

`EXPLORE OUR WORK ↓`

or

`SELECTED WORK ↓`

Keep CTA treatment typographic.

# Tone of Voice

Urca should sound:

- concise
- assured
- intelligent
- human
- technically credible

Prefer:

**We build AI-powered products around real business needs.**

Avoid:

**We leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to unlock unparalleled digital transformation.**

Prefer:

**From first idea to working product.**

Avoid:

**We are your 360-degree innovative digital transformation partner.**

---

# Language

Primary global website language:

**English**

A Romanian or Turkish localized version can be considered later if there is a clear commercial reason.

Do not compromise the English site architecture in anticipation of localization.

All content structures should nevertheless support future locale expansion.

---

# Content Rule

Every section must answer at least one of these questions:

**What does Urca make?**

**How does Urca think?**

**Why should I trust Urca to make it?**

If a section answers none of them, remove it.
